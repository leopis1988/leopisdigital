About Leopis Digital

Leopis Digital is an online shopping website operated by Leopis (Pvt) Ltd The store serves customers across Sri Lanka and lists products across household, kitchen, electronics, family, fashion, fitness and other everyday categories.

Customers can browse product details and prices on this website, then place an order through the cart. The order is sent to Leopis on WhatsApp for confirmation. Cash on delivery is available as shown on the website.

Product specifications, images, warranty coverage and return eligibility vary by item. Please check the individual product page and confirm any questions with customer support before ordering.
