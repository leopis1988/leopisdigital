Product details

The Tripod 3110 is an ultra-lightweight, 4-section aluminum travel tripod designed for smartphones and compact cameras. It features a 3-way pan head for easy 360-degree rotation and convenient quick-flip leg locks.

The Tripod 3110 is a highly portable, budget-friendly camera stand engineered for mobile content creators, vloggers, and casual photographers. Constructed from lightweight aluminum, it weighs just 420 grams and collapses down to 35 cm, making it effortless to carry in a backpack. The tripod extends to a maximum height of 102 cm and features adjustable 4-section legs with secure flip locks. It is equipped with a 3-way pan head that supports smooth panning and tilting, a built-in bubble level for precise alignment, and a quick-release mounting plate. It safely supports lightweight devices up to 1.5 kg, making it ideal for standard smartphones, action cameras, and compact mirrorless gear.
